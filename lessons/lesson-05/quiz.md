## Quiz 1
- **Type**: drag-and-drop
- **Audio**: audio/My brain is mush. Go away. I need to study.wav
- **English**: [My, brain, is, mush]. Go away. I need to study!
- **Answer**: My brain is mush
- **Korean**: 머리가 멍해요(뇌가 녹아내린 것 같아요). 저리 가요, 나 공부해야 해요!
