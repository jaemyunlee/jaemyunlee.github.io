- **BaseForm**: go to plan
- **Options**: has not gone to plan, has stayed on schedule, has broken the bank, has made ends meet
- **Korean**: 항공편 취소와 갑작스러운 폭풍우 때문에 우리 휴가는 전혀 계획대로 풀리지 않았어요.
