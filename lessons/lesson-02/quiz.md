# Lesson 2: Ordering at a Trendy Cafe Quizzes

## Quiz 1
- **Type**: fill-in-the-blank
- **Korean**: 아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 부탁드릴게요
- **English**: Can I get an iced [coffee] to go?
- **Explanation**: "to go" means for takeout.

## Quiz 2
- **Type**: multiple-choice
- **Korean**: 우유 대신 오트 밀크로 바꿀 수 있나요?
- **English**: Could you [substitute, translate, remove, multiply] oat milk for whole milk?
- **Answer**: substitute
- **Explanation**: "Substitute A for B" means using A in place of B.

## Quiz 3
- **Type**: listening
- **Audio**: audio/order.wav
- **English**: What is your most popular [pastry] today?
- **Korean**: 오늘 가장 인기 있는 베이커리 빵은 무엇인가요?
- **Explanation**: "Pastry" refers to baked goods like croissants, scones, or danishes.
