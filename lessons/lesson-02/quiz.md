# Lesson 2: Uncle Wayne's Cabin & Dry Creek Quizzes

## Quiz 1
- **Type**: multiple-choice
- **Audio**: audio/There was nothing here on the property..wav
- **English**: There was nothing here on the [property, mountain, street, pavement].
- **Answer**: property
- **Korean**: 여기 땅(부지)에는 아무것도 없었어요.
- **Explanation**: "property"는 건물이나 주택이 딸린 "토지, 부지, 부동산"을 의미합니다. 미국에서는 단독주택이나 시골의 개인 소유 땅 전체를 흔히 "property"라고 부릅니다.

## Quiz 2
- **Type**: multiple-choice
- **Audio**: audio/we managed to use a headlight and a battery for lights..wav
- **English**: We [managed to, failed to, refused to, forgot to] use a headlight and a battery for lights.
- **Answer**: managed to
- **Korean**: 전등 대신 자동차 헤드라이트와 배터리로 겨우겨우 불을 밝혔어요.
- **Explanation**: "manage to (동사원형)"는 열악하거나 어려운 상황 속에서도 "어떻게든/간신히 ~해내다"라는 뜻의 핵심 회화 표현입니다.

## Quiz 5

## Quiz 6

- **English**: We would play in the creek [all afternoon, at dawn, for a minute, every midnight] till we were like blue-lipped.

## Quiz 7

- **English**: We could only get in the creek after we got our [chores, homework, meals, exercise] done in the morning.

## Quiz 8
- **Type**: multiple-choice
- **Audio**: audio/its been a treat.wav
- **English**: It's been [a treat, a burden, a nightmare, a struggle]!
- **Answer**: a treat
- **Korean**: 정말 큰 기쁨(특별한 즐거움)이었어요!
- **Explanation**: "a treat"는 특별한 대접, 맛있는 음식뿐 아니라 "아주 기분 좋은 일, 특별한 즐거움"을 뜻하는 일상 관용구입니다 ("It's been a treat!": 참 행복하고 즐거운 시간이었어요).

## Quiz 9
- **Type**: multiple-choice
- **Audio**: audio/The creek itself is called Dry Creek not because it ran dry of water because when back in the 1800s or 1890s whenever....wav
- **English**: The creek [itself, anymore, nevertheless, meanwhile] is called Dry Creek, not because it ran dry of water, because when back in the 1800s or 1890s, whenever it was, the gold rush was going on, they never found any gold in the creek, so they named it Dry Creek.
- **Answer**: itself
- **Korean**: 개울 그 자체의 이름이 '드라이 크릭(Dry Creek)'인데, 물이 말라서가 아니라 골드러시 때 금이 전혀 발견되지 않았기 때문이에요.
- **Explanation**: 명사 뒤의 "itself"는 "그 자체, 그것 자체"를 강조하는 재귀대명사 강조용법입니다 ("The creek itself": 그 개울 자체는).

## Quiz 10
- **Type**: multiple-choice
- **Audio**: audio/our creek feeds a lake downstream..wav
- **English**: Our creek [feeds, drains, dries, bypasses] a lake downstream.
- **Answer**: feeds

## Quiz 11
- **Type**: multiple-choice
- **Audio**: audio/Its known for fishing. they stock it like three times a year..wav
- **English**: It's known for fishing. They [stock, empty, ban, freeze] it like three times a year.
- **Answer**: stock
