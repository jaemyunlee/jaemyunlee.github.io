# Lesson 3: Airport & Travel Essentials Quizzes

## Quiz 1
- **Type**: fill-in-the-blank
- **Korean**: 제 비행기 탑승구는 어디인가요?
- **English**: Where is the departure [gate] for my flight?
- **Explanation**: "departure gate" is where passengers board their flight.

## Quiz 2
- **Type**: multiple-choice
- **Korean**: 기내 수하물로 몇 개까지 반입할 수 있나요?
- **English**: How many pieces of [carry-on, checked, excess, missing] luggage can I bring onboard?
- **Answer**: carry-on
- **Explanation**: "Carry-on luggage" refers to bags brought inside the airplane cabin.

## Quiz 3
- **Type**: listening
- **Audio**: audio/travel.wav
- **English**: Please have your boarding pass and [passport] ready for inspection
- **Korean**: 검사를 위해 탑승권과 여권을 준비해 주세요
- **Explanation**: "Passport" is the travel document required for international flights.
