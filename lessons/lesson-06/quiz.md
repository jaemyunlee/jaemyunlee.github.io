## Quiz 1
- **Type**: fill-in-the-blank
- **Audio**: audio/We ended up going out and started dating and that..wav
- **English**: We [ended up] going out and started dating and that.
- **Answer**: ended up
- **Korean**: 우리는 결국 데이트를 시작하게 되었고, 그렇게 사귀게 되었죠.
