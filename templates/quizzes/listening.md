# Listening & Fill-in-the-Blank Quiz Template

Use this template to present an audio clip with missing words for users to listen and transcribe.

## Quiz Example
- **Type**: listening
- **Audio**: audio/cats.wav
- **English**: I have [cats]
- **Korean**: 나는 고양이를 키워요
- **Explanation**: Listen closely to the native pronunciation.

## Hint Behavior
- The "Hint" button reveals the first TWO letters of each word (e.g. `ca__`).
- The "Skip" button reveals the correct answer and advances.
