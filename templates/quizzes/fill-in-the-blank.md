# Fill-in-the-Blank Quiz Template

Use this template to test target vocabulary, expressions, and grammar patterns.

## Quiz Example
- **Type**: fill-in-the-blank
- **Korean**: 나는 고양이를 키워요
- **English**: I have [cats]
- **Explanation**: "cats" is the plural form of cat.

## Hint Behavior
- The "Hint" button reveals the first and last letter of each word (e.g. `c _ _ s`).
- The "Skip" button reveals the full answer and advances.
